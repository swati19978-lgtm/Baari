How it works

  1. Patients message your clinic on WhatsApp in Hindi, English or Hinglish and get a token.

  2. They see a wait time range that updates with the doctor's pace.

  3. They get a message when a few patients are ahead.

  4. Your receptionist runs the queue from one phone screen.

Who it's for

Walk-in clinics with one to three doctors and about 30 to 80 patients a day, such as general physicians, paediatricians, ENT and dermatology.

Good to know

No medical advice
Baari only handles clinic operations. Medical questions go to your clinic staff.
No app for patients
They only need WhatsApp.
No new hardware
Your receptionist only needs a phone.
